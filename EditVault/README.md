## Asset Categories

- **png-assets/cutouts/**: High-resolution cutouts including classical statues, busts, body parts, gestures, and silhouettes.
- **png-assets/stickers/**: Badges, shapes, sparkles, starbursts, fire frames, lightning effects, and particles.
- **png-assets/objects/**: High-detail objects, money bundles, cash stacks, devices, dice, clocks, hourglasses, keys, and frames.
- **vectors/illustrations/**: 3D and 2D vector-style illustrations covering business, creative, and character workflows.
- **vectors/arrows/**: Directional cues and pointers.
- **vectors/infographics/**: Candlestick charts, coin stacks, and growth infographics.
- **icons/png/**: Brand logos and functional UI icons.
- **icons/animated/**: Animated GIF icons and reaction graphics.
- **audio/background-music/**: Background music beds.
- **overlays/**: Light ray bursts, page curls, and atmospheric overlays.
- **motion-graphics/**: Pre-rendered 3D motion graphic collages.
- **ui-assets/**: Video player frames, dark UI buttons, and interface components.
- **backgrounds/**: Background scenery and environment backdrops.
- **b-roll/lifestyle/**: Lifestyle video clips and photographic cutouts.
- **editing-templates/**: Project template files for Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and CapCut.
